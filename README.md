## Projects

**[Lyora](https://github.com/azhengis/lyora)**
Flags early signs of failure in water and climate infrastructure before they turn into a claim or a spill. Live dashboard with streaming anomaly scores. Built for insurers and pipeline operators who need a risk signal.
Under the hood: streaming anomaly detection, time series feature engineering, Next.js dashboard.

**[F1 Race Outcome Predictor](https://github.com/azhengis/f1-predictor)**
Predicts finishing outcomes for Formula 1 races and, more importantly, shows why. 58.3% accuracy against a 40% baseline.
Under the hood: LightGBM with SHAP explanations, served through FastAPI.

**[SenseMap AI](https://github.com/azhengis/sensemap-ai)**
Turns environmental sound into tactile patterns so deaf and hard of hearing users can feel what is happening around them. A smoke alarm should not require hearing.
Under the hood: Audio Spectrogram Transformer fine-tuned on ESC-50, 95.6% validation accuracy.

**[Lot-to-Life](https://github.com/azhengis/lot-to-life)**
Urban parcel intelligence that scores vacant lots in Chicago for what they could realistically become. Winner, DemonHacks.
Under the hood: geospatial data pipeline, scoring model, mapping front end.
